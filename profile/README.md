**Intelligent Asset Management, Infrastructure Automation, Facilities Operations and Sustainability with IoT & AI.**

digital blanket® is a SaaS platform built to drive automation, enhance sustainability, digitize facility operations, and optimize space portfolios — turning buildings into intelligent, data-driven assets.

## 📖 Our Story

Digital Blanket® was founded with a clear purpose: to drive sustainability in the built environment and industrial facilities. We recognized that most organizations manage energy, automation, assets, and user experience through disconnected systems — leading to inefficiencies and missed opportunities.

Our response was to create a modular cloud-native platform that brings everything together — from energy optimization to facility and asset management — while enabling a modern, intuitive workplace experience. A solution where sustainability goals and ROI go hand in hand.

## 🧩 Our Platform

A **Unified Real Estate Solution** that brings asset management, energy, maintenance, and workplace experience onto a single, scalable, and secure enterprise-class platform — with **no rip & replace** required for existing infrastructure.
